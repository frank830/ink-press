Ink Press · Support

Player support: wuhoodev@gmail.com

An offline cube puzzle. Free to play, with all 24 boards available. No account, ads or in-app purchases.

Roll, then stamp

Roll toward an adjacent tile using a diagonal swipe or one of the four arrow buttons. The arrows follow the isometric board. Rolling changes the cube’s position and the orientation of its six faces; it does not leave an impression.

Stamp prints every inked side onto its adjacent empty tile and uses the ink on that face. The top and bottom do not print. Match every outlined target within the move budget. Small preview dots show the landing tiles; circles, bars and crosses distinguish the three inks without relying on colour alone.

A wrong impression ends the attempt. Undo restores the previous action and has no limit. Restart resets the current attempt while keeping completed-board records. The top-left board selector opens all 24 boards. Selecting the current board preserves its attempt.

Inspect the board and cube

Tap the six-face strip to inspect ink on hidden faces. Open How to play → Inspect board, or use Inspect board in the face inspector, for a text list of every tile, hole, target, impression and cube position. The board also supports the standard accessibility activation to open this map.

Sound, language and saves

Settings contains English/Simplified Chinese, sound effects and haptics. If you cannot hear effects, check the sound switch, system volume and Silent Mode. There is no background music. System Reduce Motion is supported.

Progress, best move counts, the current attempt and settings are stored on this device. There is no game account, server backup or cross-device sync. Deleting the app removes its local data; system device backups may retain a copy according to your settings.

Report a problem

Email wuhoodev@gmail.com with the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, board number, steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results. A game screenshot is optional. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

滚动盖章 · 玩家支持

离线方块解谜游戏。免费开放全部 24 关，无账号、广告或内购。

先滚动，再盖章

朝相邻地砖的方向斜滑，或使用四个方向按钮。箭头方向与等距棋盘一致。滚动会改变方块的位置和六面朝向，不会留下印记。

盖章时，所有有墨的侧面会同时印到相邻空格，盖过的那面就没有墨了。顶面和底面不会盖章。请在步数限制内对上全部目标轮廓。小色点预览落墨位置；圆点、双线、十字让你不必只靠颜色辨认墨水。

印错地砖会结束这次尝试。撤销恢复上一步，次数不限。重开只重置当前尝试，保留已完成记录。左上角可选择全部 24 关；再次选择当前关卡会保留这次尝试。

查看棋盘与方块

轻点六面墨水条，查看隐藏面的墨水。在「玩法 → 查看棋盘」或六面详情中的「查看棋盘」，可逐格查看空洞、目标、印记和方块位置。棋盘也支持通过标准辅助功能激活操作打开文字详情。

音效、语言与存档

设置中可切换简体中文与英文、音效及触感。听不到音效时，请检查游戏音效开关、系统音量和静音模式。游戏没有背景音乐，并支持系统的「减弱动态效果」。

进度、最佳步数、当前尝试和设置保存在本机，没有游戏账号、服务器备份或跨设备同步。删除应用会移除本机数据；系统设备备份可能按照你的设置保留副本。
