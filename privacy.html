Ink Press · Privacy

Effective November 16, 2026.

Ink Press does not collect or transmit personal data. It runs offline without accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, third-party service SDKs or network requests.

Data stays on your device

The app saves game progress, best move counts, the current board and action history, and language, sound and haptic preferences in its private local storage. If an attempt cannot be restored, valid preferences and completed-board records are retained where possible. Unreadable or unsupported save data may be kept locally for recovery.

This storage is not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. System backups may retain a copy according to your device settings. The developer does not hold an online game account or server-side gameplay record to delete. There is no cross-device sync.

If you contact support

If you choose to email player support, your email provider processes the message and the developer receives the details you voluntarily include to answer your request. This is separate from the offline game. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Support and privacy questions: wuhoodev@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy describes Ink Press 1.0. If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated.

滚动盖章 · 隐私说明

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日。

Ink Press 不收集或传输个人数据。游戏离线运行，无账号、广告、分析服务、追踪、第三方服务 SDK 或网络请求。

数据保存在你的设备上

应用会在私有本地存储中保存游戏进度、最佳步数、当前关卡与操作历史，以及语言、音效和触感偏好。无法恢复一次尝试时，会尽可能保留有效偏好和已完成记录。无法读取或不支持的存档可能保留在本机，以便恢复。

这些存储不会发送给开发者。删除应用会移除此设备上的本地数据；系统备份可能按照你的设备设置保留副本。开发者没有可供删除的在线游戏账号或服务器游戏记录，也不提供跨设备同步。

主动联系玩家支持

如果你自愿发送支持邮件，邮箱服务会处理该邮件，开发者会为回复请求而收到你主动提供的内容。这与离线游戏的数据独立。请避免发送不必要的个人信息。

支持及隐私问题请联系：wuhoodev@gmail.com。

说明更新

本说明对应 Ink Press 1.0。如果应用的数据处理方式改变，本页面会随之更新。
